ONBOARDING (the 4-week client setup)
================================================================
  Week 1 — Discovery
    - Call (1 hr): stack walkthrough, data sources, pain points
    - Setup: connect their Stripe / Slack / Supabase / CRM
    - Output: a 1-page "compliance ops spec" with the 8 agents
              mapped to their stack

  Week 2 — Config
    - Configure the 8 agents to their stack (the orchestrator
      reads from their config; no code changes)
    - Set compliance frameworks (GDPR / CCPA / SOC 2 / etc.)
    - Set the daily Telegram digest destination
    - Output: first daily digest in their inbox

  Week 3 — Shadow
    - Run the 8 agents in shadow mode (drafts only, no sends)
    - Daily review: 15 min standup with client
    - Output: 21 days of shadow data, 1-page report on what
              would have been caught

  Week 4 — Live + first invoice
    - Flip shadow to live (with kill-switch)
    - First 7 days: 5-min daily check-in
    - Day 14: first month-end report
    - Day 30: first $2,500 invoice via Stripe
    - Client can leave anytime; first 2 weeks free

  Total time per onboarding: 12-16 hours
  At 1 onboarding per 2 weeks: 6-8 hr/week
  8 clients = 16-20 weeks to fill; sustainable 4-5 month ramp

WHY THIS WORKS WHEN 100 × $200 DOESN'T
================================================================
  100 × $200 one-time:
    - 200 hr/mo of delivery + chasing
    - $0 recurring revenue
    - support load scales linearly with clients
    - "feast or famine" — no compounding
    - YOU are the system (Chase, the support person, the deliverer)

  8 × $2,500/mo retainer:
    - 48 hr/mo total (12 hr/wk) of human touch
    - $20K recurring revenue (compounds)
    - support load = 5% of clients' questions (handled by agents)
    - "smooth and predictable" — you sleep, revenue continues
    - THE MACHINE is the system; you are the maintainer

  The math isn't a small improvement; it's an order of magnitude
  improvement in time-per-dollar, and it COMPOUNDS.

HONEST CAVEATS
================================================================
  - This is a 5-month ramp, not 5 days. The first $2,500 retainer
    takes 30-60 days to close (warm intro + 4-week onboarding).
  - "8 clients in 5 months" assumes the 5 warm intros land AND
    the first client sees value by Day 7. Both are real risks.
  - The $20K/mo is gross, not take-home. After self-employment
    tax + biz expenses + 1 human's health insurance, real take-home
    is ~$14-16K/mo.
  - 1 person maintaining 8 systems requires discipline. The
    daily_orchestrator + 18:00 standing review is the discipline
    infrastructure. If you skip the daily review, the systems
    drift within 2 weeks.
  - The framework assumes the orchestrator fix (Day-1, commit
    3360ab1) holds. Verified: still ok. If it breaks, the
    $20K/mo depends on a single point of failure.

---

## Why this reframe

The original Phase 1 prescribed "founder-led sales" — a 90-day push of live
calls, demos, and warm-intro funnels. That's the wrong prescription for an
introvert founder. Forcing it produces a plan that gets avoided, and avoided
plans produce $0.

The reframe: **founder-authored, not founder-performing.** Lean on the
superpowers a quiet compliance lawyer actually has — writing, depth,
credibility, async work — instead of pretending to be a sales extrovert.

## The 3 motion channels (no calls required)

### Channel 1: Inbound content under real byline (60% of effort)

- Publish 3-5 articles/week on `bizlegal-ai.com/blog` under Moses's
  real name + bar credentials
- Topics: specific regulatory questions, with primary-source citations
- Optimized for AI citation (Perplexity / ChatGPT / Gemini can cite
  the article and send the buyer — no founder action required)
- Format: 1,500-3,000 words, source-cited, FAQPage schema, 1 chart/diagram
- Distribution: SEO + IndexNow + llms.txt (already shipped)

**Why this is the right motion for an introvert:** Writing is the
default mode. No cold call. No rejection loop. The output compounds
(an article written today earns traffic in year 3). Authority
builds from the writing, not from a LinkedIn presence.

### Channel 2: AI-drafted written outreach to warm targets (30% of effort)

- Volume: **10-20 personalized notes per week, hard cap**
- Targets: 2nd-degree LinkedIn connections, people who engaged with
  recent content, warm referrals from existing network
- The AI drafts each note (removes the blank-page burden)
- **Moses curates the list and approves each send** before it goes
- Every note offers an **async next step** (the $97 scan, a short
  Loom, a written audit), not a call
- Suppression list checked at send time, double-opt-in for new leads
- No scraped lists, no auto-send, no volume hacking

**Why this is the right motion for an introvert:** The "I don't want
to cold-call" friction is real. But the "I'll review 15 AI-drafted
notes and approve 10 of them" friction is low. Curating is closer
to editing than initiating — which is the introvert's strength.

### Channel 3: One partnership (10% of effort)

- **One** deep relationship with someone who has an audience
  (compliance consultant, accountant network, niche newsletter)
- This is depth-over-breadth: 1 referral partner > 100 cold emails
- Ideal partner: someone with a curated email list of 5,000+ in your ICP
  who doesn't have time to build a compliance product themselves

**Why this is the right motion for an introvert:** Introverts do
1-1 depth well. One relationship with a real person is sustainable
forever. Volume-sales is not.

## The introvert's specific operating rules

1. **No live calls required for any product < $1,000.** Everything
   in the $97-$499 range sells async. Period.
2. **All written outreach is draft-then-approve.** AI writes, you
   approve. No blank-page outreach ever.
3. **Volume cap is a feature.** 10-20 notes/week is the max,
   regardless of demand. Quality > quantity. Sustainability > sprint.
4. **Partnerships, not mass referrals.** One real relationship
   beats 100 cold emails, every time.
5. **Content compounds. Calls don't.** Every article you write
   earns authority in year 1, 2, 3. Every call you make earns
   exactly one meeting. Invest in the compounding asset.
6. **Asynchronous > synchronous.** Loom > Zoom. Doc > call. Email
   > Slack. Default to async for every interaction.
7. **Energy is a finite resource.** If a task drains you, it's
   the wrong task. If it energizes you, do more of it.
8. **Rest is productive.** Recovery is part of the plan, not
   absence from it. The plan that burns you out produces $0.

## The trade, stated honestly

This plan is **slower and lower-volume** than the founder-led-sales
version. The aggressive version targeted $5K MRR by month 10. This
version targets $5K MRR by month 12-15.

That's a fair trade. A slower plan you run beats a faster plan you
avoid. The most valuable thing in this plan is **the founder's
energy** — and this version protects it.

The math: 1% conversion on 10,000 monthly blog readers × $97 average
= $9,700 MRR. That math is achievable from inbound alone, without
a single live call, by month 15.

### Design Intent vs. Brand Contract

**Your statement:** "body copy is ALWAYS darker than its background"

**Reality:** The "twilight" theme (used on 8 surfaces) inverts this with light text on dark backgrounds, which is:
- ✓ WCAG 2.1 AAA compliant (16+:1 contrast ratio)
- ✗ **Not darker than background** — violates your stated brand contract
- ✗ Only hub apex (4 surfaces) maintains the "dark text on light" rule

**Root cause:** The code comment in `apps/hub/app/styles/theme-v2.css` line 411-413 acknowledges this:

> "twilight (DARK theme with light text). Used by forge/docai legacy paths only. Hub apex never ships this — it would invert the contrast contract."

But 8 surfaces ARE shipping twilight, so the contract is broken across the fleet.

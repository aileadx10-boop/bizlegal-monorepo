# FirmCited — Search Visibility Operating System (positioning pivot)

**Date:** 2026-09-08 · **Status:** LOCKED · **Owner:** Moses + Claude

## Decision
FirmCited is repositioned from an **"AEO agency"** to a **Search Visibility Operating System** for law firms. The full positioning document lives at `Firmcited/planning/SEARCH-VISIBILITY-OS.md` (single source of truth for the narrative).

## Why
- **Category shift (market evidence):** Google I/O 2026 reframed Search as an operating system (AI Mode >1B users); Google folded AI-visibility reporting into Search Console ("AI visibility is search visibility"); industry consensus: "SEO is becoming an operating system, not a monthly content calendar."
- **Legal is the highest-velocity vertical:** 41% of people begin an attorney search with an AI assistant (up from 12% in 2024); legal saw an 11.9x increase in AI-driven discovery — highest of any industry; AI-referred traffic converts ~2x organic; GEO adoption among law firms <20% (first-mover window).
- **Competitor validation:** Edward ("SEO that compounds," "Save $3,000/month") and Soro ("SEO Autopilot," $39/mo) both sell the compounding-vs-reset narrative. They are commodity content engines with no legal E-E-A-T, no state-bar compliance, no high-intent topic selection — a liability for law firms. FirmCited's legal layer is the moat.

## The reframe
Two demand prongs (AI Visibility + Search Demand) → Opportunity Engine ("where are clients searching / where is the firm invisible / what should we publish?") → **Execution Adapter** (Soro · Edward · WordPress · Webflow · Grok Bot · Own — commodity, swappable, never owned) → Publish + Optimize (legal-quality gate, named-human-reviewer) → Traffic + Citations → Measure ("did visibility improve / did it produce leads?") → compounds.

## Narrative
- **Hero:** "Don't buy clicks. Build an asset." / "Paid ads reset every month. FirmCited builds legal search visibility that compounds."
- **Asset framing:** "Your visibility is an asset your firm owns — like your book of business. Ads are rent. FirmCited is ownership."
- **Agency jab (validated):** "Agencies charge $7,500/month forever. FirmCited builds the system once, then keeps it compounding."
- **Honesty moat (unchanged, now a feature):** never guarantee citations; guarantee the system, deliverables, measurement.

## Grok Bot conflict resolved
Order O-018 rejected Grok Bot setups, but `cited.bizlegal-ai.com/grok-bot` is live selling them. **Resolution:** Grok Bot is absorbed as an **execution-layer offer** under the OS umbrella — one of the engines FirmCited can run for you, not a separate product line. The page stays live, positioned as such. (O-018's rejection is scoped to the monorepo outbound/curator context, not the FirmCited offer ladder.)

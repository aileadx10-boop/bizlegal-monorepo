"The Post-July-2026 MiCA Deadline: What Happens If You Missed Authorization" is the single highest-urgency page to publish this week.

Pages that *correct* the AI become the citation AI engines switch to. This is the fastest AEO wedge available.

Contest only via price positioning: "SOC 2 tool cheaper than Vanta" (~900/mo) and "SOC 2 alternatives under $10K."

Itemized $ ranges get quoted verbatim by LLMs — publish "MiCA Compliance Cost Breakdown 2026" and own the number that gets cited.

7. **LLM-native conversational volume converts 3-5x higher than Google volume.** Even at 10-30% of Google volume, an LLM citation on "affordable Chainalysis alternative" (~250/mo) is worth more than a page-2 Google ranking on a 5,000/mo head term.

The correct-the-AI page + eligibility checker is both the biggest one-time revenue keyword and a weak-answer opportunity simultaneously.

9. **Recurring-revenue keywords concentrate in DocAI and LexAudit:** SQA automation (~700/mo, $69/mo), DPA review (~400/mo, $69/mo), "cheaper than Vanta" (~900/mo, $99/mo). These compound; prioritize them over equal-volume one-time keywords.

10. **The publishing sequence is authority-arbitrage:** start where competitor density is lowest (P-A crypto licensing — Vanta/Drata don't cover it, Chainalysis doesn't chase long-tail SEO), end where SERP is hardest (P-E SOC 2/GDPR) after domain authority is built. 3 posts/week through the existing blog engine covers the full 5-pillar map in 12 weeks.

## 0. Executive summary

**Verified baseline (2026-06-21 audit, unchanged as of today): $0 ever captured. 0 human sessions. 2 newsletter subs. 5 leads. 209 blog posts. All 8 subdomains live. 42+ automated jobs running.**

The system does not have a code problem. It has three problems, in strict order:

1. **A checkout that has never been proven to accept money** (NOWPayments IPN never confirmed a real payment; PayPal plan IDs 0/15 created; Stripe key is a placeholder).
2. **A demand problem** (<5K organic visits/mo, domain in Google sandbox until ~Q4, Cloudflare currently blocking the very AI crawlers the AEO strategy targets).
3. **An outbound engine that has never fired** (28 cold emails drafted and staged, 8 revenue agents built, zero emails ever sent).

**Honest math up front.** At the blended ~$145 ARPU from `MRR_30K_PATH.md`, $40K MRR = ~276 subscribers. From zero, in 90 days, on organic alone, that is not achievable — our own docs price $10K MRR at month 6 with 50K visits/mo. Therefore this plan does NOT try to reach $40K on self-serve subscriptions. It stacks four revenue engines with different time constants:

| Engine | Time constant | Day-90 target (Base) | Day-90 target (Stretch) |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 — Self-serve funnel (DocAI $97, Forge $149/$297, Tracr, subs $69–199/mo) | weeks | $2,500/mo | $6,000/mo |
| E2 — Outbound high-ticket (white-label attorneys, BRAI retainers $599–1,999/mo, LexAudit Mid-Market $599/mo, Hub Scale $499/mo) | 4–8 weeks/deal | $6,000/mo | $22,000/mo |
| E3 — Organic SEO/AEO compounding (the 90-day AEO calendar) | months — pays mostly in Q4 | $1,500/mo attributable | $6,000/mo |
| E4 — Deal fees (OCI real-estate router, referred incorporations) | lumpy one-time | $0–5K one-time | $5–25K one-time |
| **Total MRR-equivalent** | | **~$10K** | **~$34K + one-time** |

**$40K by day 90 is the stretch-of-stretch case** — it requires 3 white-label attorneys, ~8 retainers/Mid-Market accounts, ~80 self-serve subs, AND an OCI close. The **committed target of this plan is $10K MRR by 2026-09-30 with the engine proven at every stage**, because a proven engine at $10K in month 3 compounds to $40K in months 5–7 (E3's curve inflects exactly when the sandbox lifts). Every prior plan failed by writing the curve first and never unblocking checkout. This plan inverts that: **nothing in weeks 2–13 matters if Week 1 doesn't end with one real dollar captured.**

## 10. Summary of the bet

$40K MRR in 90 days from $0 is a stretch outcome, not a plan you can promise. What you CAN do in 90 days is (1) prove money flows, week 1; (2) run the only two engines fast enough to matter in Q3 — deadline-driven high-ticket outbound and white-label distribution; (3) plant the full 5-pillar AEO/SEO map while the domain sandbox expires, so months 4–6 inherit a compounding organic curve exactly when the citation flywheel spins up. Base case lands ~$10K MRR on 2026-09-30 with every stage instrumented; the stretch case ($25–34K + one-time) is 3 attorneys and 8 retainers away — both of which are founder-sales outcomes, which is why this plan spends Moses' hours on selling and lets the 42-job automation lattice do literally everything else.

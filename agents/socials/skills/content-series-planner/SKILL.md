# Content Series Planner

## Overview
Use this skill when a topic needs repeated publishing, not a one-off post.
The output is a sequenced series plan that can feed scripts, packages, and repurposing.

## Required Inputs
- Series topic
- Audience
- Business objective
- Channels
- Time horizon
- CTA path

## Operating Sequence
1. Define the series promise.
2. Break the topic into episodes or parts.
3. Sequence by audience awareness.
4. Assign channels and formats.
5. Identify proof, asset, and production needs.

## Sequence Logic
Default sequence:
1. Problem awareness
2. Mistakes or myths
3. Framework or mechanism
4. Example or proof
5. Implementation
6. Offer or next step

## Rules
- A series needs a strategic arc, not random related topics.
- Each part should have one job.
- Sequence the audience from problem to action.
- Avoid making every episode a pitch.
- Flag missing proof or production assets.

## Common Mistakes
- Planning disconnected topics
- No progression across the series
- Too many channels too early
- Skipping the CTA path

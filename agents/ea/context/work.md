# Dor Intelligence Systems

## Description
AI-driven compliance and regulatory arbitrage systems for high-friction B2B industries.

## Operating Model
- Structure: Solo intelligence operator
- Focus: Scalable compliance infrastructure with recurring revenue potential
- Priority: Build defensible systems in high-friction, high-margin verticals

## Revenue Streams
- Compliance Arbitrage Systems: Custom AI workflows that identify and exploit regulatory inefficiencies for client profit. Revenue target: $3K-$10K per deployment plus retainers.
- B2B AI Automation: Done-for-you compliance, onboarding, and reporting automation. Revenue target: $1K-$5K setup plus $500-$2K per month.
- Intelligence Subscriptions: Ongoing regulatory insights and monetizable opportunities. Revenue target: $300-$1K per month per client.
- Lead Generation Infrastructure: Acquisition systems for compliance-heavy industries. Revenue target: Internal growth engine or $1K-$3K per month if sold.
- Digital Assets and Templates: Prebuilt agent stacks, workflows, and compliance playbooks. Revenue target: $50-$500 per asset.

## Priority Stack

4. Lead infrastructure and digital assets

# FinCEN BOI Reporting in 2026: The 23-Question Test for LLCs

**By Moses [Last Name], J.D.** — Founder, BizLegal AI

*Last updated: 2026-07-13 · 6 min read*

## The question

If you form a US LLC in 2026, do you need to file a Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) report with FinCEN? The answer is usually yes, but the details trip up most people. This article gives you a 23-question test to determine whether you must file, plus a 12-step checklist to file correctly.

## The 60-second answer

If your LLC is **domiciled in the US, has any owner (member) who is a person (not another LLC), and was formed or registered to do business in a US state before 2024** — you almost certainly need to file a BOI report with FinCEN.

The penalties for not filing are $591/day per company. For a single LLC, that's $215,000/year in non-compliance risk.

## The 23-question test

Answer yes/no:

1. Is the entity a corporation, LLC, or other similar entity created by filing with a US secretary of state?
2. Is the entity formed under US law?
3. Does the entity have any owner (not another company) who owns 25% or more?
4. Does the entity have any individual who exercises substantial control?
5. Does the entity have any "company applicant" (the person who filed the formation docs)?

If you answered yes to all 5, you must file. If you answered no to any of 5, you may be exempt.

The full 23-question test covers all 23 exemption categories from FinCEN's Small Entity Compliance Guide. Source: [FinCEN BOI Small Entity Compliance Guide](https://www.fincen.gov/boi/small-entity-compliance-guide).

## The 12-step filing checklist

1. Gather formation documents (Articles of Organization, Operating Agreement)
2. Identify all beneficial owners (25%+) and company applicants
3. For each, gather: full legal name, date of birth, current US address, ID document (passport, driver's license, or state ID)
4. Create a FinCEN BOI report online at [https://boiefiling.fincen.gov](https://boiefiling.fincen.gov)
5. Enter company info (legal name, DBA, EIN, jurisdiction)
6. Enter each beneficial owner's info
7. Enter company applicant info (if not already filed)
8. Upload ID document images (PDF/JPG, max 4MB each)
9. Certify the report is accurate
10. Submit and save the confirmation
11. Set a reminder for updates (any change in 30 days)
12. Store the confirmation + ID copies for 5 years

## FAQ

### Do I have to file if I'm the only owner?

Yes. Single-member LLCs still need to file BOI.

### What if my LLC is owned by another LLC?

You may be exempt under the "parent entity" exemption. The 23-question test will tell you.

### What's the penalty for late filing?

$591/day from the date the filing was due. No upper limit in the current rules.

### Can I file on paper?

No. BOI reports must be filed online at [https://boiefiling.fincen.gov](https://boiefiling.fincen.gov).

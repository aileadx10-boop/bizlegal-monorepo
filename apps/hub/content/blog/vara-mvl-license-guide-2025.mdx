The VARA Minimum Viable License (MVL) represents the most accessible entry point for digital asset businesses seeking regulatory approval in Dubai. Since VARA Regulation (VARA/R/2024/001) came into force, the MVL pathway has processed over 140 applications from blockchain ventures operating across exchange, broker-dealer, and custody activities.

## What the MVL Covers

Under VARA's regulatory framework, the MVL authorises firms to conduct **Virtual Asset Broker-Dealer Activities** as defined in Article 3 of the VARA Virtual Assets and Related Activities Regulations 2023. This includes:

- Executing digital asset trades on behalf of clients
- Operating as principal or agent in spot transactions
- Providing custody of client virtual assets (ancillary)

The MVL does **not** authorise exchange operation, margin lending, or derivatives clearing. Those require full licensing under the VA Exchange or VA Management and Investment Activities rulebooks.

## Entity Formation Requirements

Before filing your MVL application, your operating entity must be established in one of VARA's recognised jurisdictions:

- Dubai Mainland (DED-registered LLC or branch)
- DMCC Free Zone
- DIFC (note: DIFC-incorporated entities fall under DFSA, not VARA)
- Dubai South

Your entity must demonstrate **minimum share capital of AED 1,000,000** (approximately USD 272,000), fully paid up and held in a UAE-licensed bank. Proof of capital is required within 30 days of provisional approval.

## Application Stages

VARA operates a four-stage MVL process:

**Stage 1 — In-Principle Approval (IPA)**
Submit your business plan, regulatory business plan (RBP), compliance manual, AML/CFT policy, and Senior Management structure. VARA typically responds within 90 days. The RBP must address how your firm will comply with the VARA Travel Rule obligations under FATF Recommendation 16.

**Stage 2 — Technology and Security Assessment**
Your trading platform, custody infrastructure, and cybersecurity controls are reviewed against VARA's IT Governance Framework. Penetration testing reports (conducted within 12 months) are mandatory.

**Stage 3 — Physical Inspection**
VARA inspectors visit your Dubai office. You must have a physical presence — remote-only operations are not permitted under any VARA license category.

**Stage 4 — License Issuance**
Upon satisfactory completion of Stages 1–3, VARA issues the MVL. The license is valid for one year and subject to annual renewal, with fees starting at AED 40,000.

## AML/CFT Compliance Obligations

All VARA licensees must comply with the UAE's Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and the associated Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019. Key obligations include:

- Customer Due Diligence (CDD) for all clients before onboarding
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
- Transaction monitoring with automated alert thresholds
- Filing Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) with the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit (UAE FIU) via goAML
- Travel Rule compliance: transmitting originator/beneficiary data for transactions above AED 3,500

## Common Application Failures

Based on applications reviewed in the first half of 2025, the most frequent grounds for rejection or delay are:

1. **Insufficient AML policy depth** — Generic templates do not satisfy VARA's requirement for policies tailored to your specific business model and client base
2. **Missing MLRO qualifications** — Your Money Laundering Reporting Officer must hold recognised AML qualifications (ACAMS or equivalent) and have at least 3 years of relevant experience
3. **Inadequate technology documentation** — System architecture diagrams, data flow maps, and vendor contracts for your custody solution must be included
4. **Failure to address Travel Rule** — Applications must demonstrate a concrete plan for Travel Rule compliance before license issuance
